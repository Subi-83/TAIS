Think of photosynthesis as the way plants "cook" their own food. Since plants can’t go to a grocery store, they use a chemical process to turn raw ingredients from nature into energy.

Here is the simple breakdown of how it works:

### 1. The Ingredients
To make food, a plant needs three basic things:
*   **Sunlight:** This is the energy source (like the heat from a stove).
*   **Water:** Absorbed from the ground through the roots.
*   **Carbon Dioxide:** A gas that plants breathe in from the air (the same gas humans breathe out).
