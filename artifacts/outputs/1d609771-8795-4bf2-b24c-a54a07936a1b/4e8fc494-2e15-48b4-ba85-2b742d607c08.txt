A computer system is a complex integration of hardware and software components working together to process data into meaningful information. These components can be categorized into four primary functional areas:

### 1. The Central Processing Unit (CPU)
Often referred to as the "brain" of the computer, the CPU is responsible for executing instructions and performing calculations. It consists of three sub-components:
*   **Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU):** Performs all mathematical calculations (addition, subtraction) and logical operations (AND, OR, NOT).
*   **Control Unit (CU):** Acts as the "manager," directing the flow of data between the CPU and other hardware components, and ensuring instructions are executed in the correct sequence.
*   **Registers:** Small, high-speed storage locations used to hold immediate data and instructions currently being processed.
