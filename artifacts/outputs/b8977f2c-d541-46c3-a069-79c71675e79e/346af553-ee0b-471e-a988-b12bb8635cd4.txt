Starting a backyard garden is one of the most rewarding ways to connect with nature, improve your diet, and save money. However, many beginners fail because they treat gardening like a one-time task rather than a biological process.

This guide follows a **"Foundation-First"** approach, ensuring you build a garden that thrives year after year, rather than just surviving the first month.

---

### Phase 1: The Blueprint (Planning)
Before you pick up a shovel, you must evaluate your environment. A garden's success is 80% preparation and 20% execution.

**1. Location, Location, Location**
*   **Sunlight:** Most vegetables need **6–8 hours of direct sunlight**. Observe your yard throughout the day. A spot that looks sunny at 10:00 AM might be shaded by a fence or tree by 2:00 PM.
