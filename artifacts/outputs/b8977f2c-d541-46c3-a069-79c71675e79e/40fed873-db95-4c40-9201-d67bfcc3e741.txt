### 1. The Architecture of Communication: From Nuance to Brevity
Social media has fundamentally altered the *mechanics* of how we exchange information.

*   **Asynchronous Communication and the "Edit" Culture:** Unlike face-to-face conversation, which requires real-time processing of tone, body language, and immediate response, social media allows for "curated asynchronicity." We can pause, delete, and refine our words. While this empowers those who struggle with social anxiety, it can erode the ability to handle spontaneous, high-stakes emotional friction, leading to a preference for "safe" communication.
