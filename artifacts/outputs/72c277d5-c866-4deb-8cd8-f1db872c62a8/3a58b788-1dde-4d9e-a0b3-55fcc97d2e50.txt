To understand photosynthesis, think of it as the way plants **cook their own food.** 

Since plants can’t go to a grocery store, they have to make their own energy using three basic "ingredients" from the environment.

Here is the simple breakdown:

### 1. The Ingredients
To make food, a plant needs three things:
*   **Sunlight:** This is the energy (the "stove").
*   **Water:** Absorbed from the ground through the roots.
*   **Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$):** A gas that plants breathe in from the air (the same gas humans breathe out).
